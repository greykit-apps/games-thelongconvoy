# The Long Convoy

**A wolfpack against Pumacity's convoys, or an escort against the wolfpack.** A remake of *Wolfpack* (Broderbund, 1990, DOS): command a pack of Wolf Empire U-boats against the convoys of Pumacity, or sail with the convoy's escorts and hunt them, in modern hi-bit pixel art, rigged for red light.

**Play:** [greykit.com/games/thelongconvoy](https://greykit.com/games/thelongconvoy/)

![The title: a destroyer steaming at dusk, a U-boat running under her in a dark red sea, and the menu on an engine-room telegraph](screenshots/title.png)

## How it plays

Pumacity's convoys carry its oil, ore and troops between the islands, and the Pumacity Defense Fleet's escorts ring them round. The Wolf Empire has a pack. Every boat in it sails under the order you give her from the map: attack, shadow, patrol, evade, charge her batteries, hold. Take command of one at a time and the dashboard is hers: the periscope or the bridge, the sonar's waterfall, the dials, her tubes. Select a ship, wait for the lamp to say on target, and fire the spread; then go deep and quiet while the escorts hunt you. The rest of the pack fights on under their own captains, and their news comes in by name.

Or play the other side, as the 1990 game let you: aboard one of the escorts, against a pack that sails on its own. Sprint, then stop and listen; ping, run in over the contact and lay a pattern of depth charges set to its depth; give the other escorts their orders from the map.

Eight missions from the pack's side and eight from the escorts', from two boats against five unescorted ships to the decisive battle, plus a custom mission and target practice. Each ends with the 1990 game's debrief: a score, a rating, and every vessel's fate.

**Reality** settings, chosen before a mission, make the war harder (limited visibility, zig-zags, expert escorts, torpedo failures, port-only repairs). **Classic** plays as 1990 did: the instruments and your eyes. **Field**, the default, adds a solution line, torpedo tracks, range labels and key hints.

## Keys

| | |
|---|---|
| `1`–`5` | dashboard, map, sonar, damage, the captain's log |
| `W` `S` / `A` `D` | telegraph / helm |
| `Q` `E` | depth; `P` periscope depth, `U` surface, `C` crash dive |
| `Enter` / `N` / `O` | take command of the boat selected / of the next / her next order |
| `T` / `Space` | select the ship nearest the crosshair / fire the spread |
| `X` | silent running; on an escort, active or passive sonar |
| `+` `−` | time compression |
| `Esc` / `L` / `M` / `?` | pause / Classic or Field / sound / every key |

A desktop game for keyboard, mouse or gamepad; Help has every key and the pad's map.

## Saves

Kept in the browser's `localStorage` under keys starting `thelongconvoy_`. **Settings → Forget everything** clears them.
